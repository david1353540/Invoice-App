# Third-party components

Browser dependencies are bundled for offline use. Keep their accompanying licences when redistributing this repository:

- Tesseract.js: `outputs/invoice-studio/vendor/ocr/LICENSE.md` and the adjacent `.LICENSE.txt` notices.
- Tesseract OCR core and English language data: `vendor/ocr/core/LICENSE` and `vendor/ocr/lang/LICENSE` under the static directory.

Ollama and AI model weights are downloaded separately and are not repository contents. Their own licences and terms apply: [Ollama](https://github.com/ollama/ollama), [NuExtract3](https://huggingface.co/numind/NuExtract3), [Qwen3.5 9B](https://ollama.com/library/qwen3.5:9b).
